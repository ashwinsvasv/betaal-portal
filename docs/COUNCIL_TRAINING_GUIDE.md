# Sunwai Council Member Training Guide

A one-page operational handbook for IIM Lucknow Student Council role owners.

---

## 1. Core Principle: Accountability & Deadlines
Every issue submitted on Sunwai has a single **Owner** and a clear **Deadline**. Students and the administration track this accountability live on every ticket via the colored accountability pill.

### The 48-Hour Acknowledgment Clock
- When an issue is raised and routed to your role, a **48-hour countdown starts immediately**.
- Within 48 hours, you must log in to your **My Inbox** and either:
  1. **Acknowledge**: Accept ownership and let the student know you have seen it.
  2. **Redirect**: If it belongs to another council portfolio, redirect it with an explanatory note (maximum 2 redirects allowed per ticket).
  3. **Reject with reason**: If the issue is invalid, duplicate, or out of council scope.
- **What happens if you miss 48 hours?**
  - At hour 48: The issue turns red and automatically escalates to the **Student Council President**.
  - At hour 96: If still unacknowledged, it escalates to the **Dean of Student Affairs**. Escalations reflect directly in the public council accountability report.

---

## 2. Navigating "My Inbox"
When logged in with your council role, the header displays **My inbox** with three tabs:
1. **Needs action**: Tickets assigned to you that are *Raised* (awaiting your 48h response) or *In Progress* (where a weekly update is due).
2. **Waiting on student**: Tickets marked *Completed/Fixed* awaiting student verification (students have 7 days to confirm or reopen).
3. **Done**: Fully resolved or closed tickets.

---

## 3. Taking Action on an Issue

### Acknowledging an Issue
- Open the issue from your inbox.
- In the **Action panel**, write a brief, reassuring note to the student (e.g. *"Inspected the water heater switchboard in Wing B; vendor technician contacted."*).
- Click **Acknowledge**. An automated email is dispatched to the student immediately.

### Starting Work & Posting Weekly Updates
- When active repair/negotiation begins, click **Start work**.
- **Weekly update rule**: In-progress issues require a progress update at least once every **7 days**. If 7 days elapse without an update, the issue is flagged as overdue.
- Attach proof photos whenever physical work or vendor invoices are available.

### Marking Fixed
- Once the problem has been solved, write resolution notes, optionally upload a photo proof of the fix, and click **Mark fixed**.
- The student is notified and given **7 days** to verify. If the student confirms, the ticket closes. If unverified after 7 days, the ticket closes automatically. If the student reopens it, it returns to your *Needs action* queue.

### Redirecting an Issue
- If an issue was misrouted to you (e.g., an IT network issue tagged under Infrastructure), expand **"Not yours? Redirect it"** in the action panel.
- Select the appropriate council role and provide a clear reason.
- The 48-hour acknowledgment clock restarts for the new owner. A ticket can only be redirected twice before being locked to the President.

---

## 4. Privacy & Safety Guidelines
- **Public issues**: Visible to all registered students, voting enabled.
- **Private issues**: Confidential personal/financial issues. Visible ONLY to the student raiser, the assigned council owner, and the Council President. (Tech administrators cannot view private complaint contents).
- **Harassment & Safety**: If any ticket involves sexual harassment, discrimination, or immediate physical safety, **do not handle it via Sunwai**. Immediately redirect the student to the **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)** or **Campus Security (ext. 2222)**.
